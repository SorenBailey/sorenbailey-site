Before you join

Does membership guarantee I’ll get a property?

No. Deal availability varies, and an early look doesn’t reserve a property. Each purchase has its own review, proof-of-funds, contract, and closing requirements.

Is the 1% listing fee my entire cost to sell?

No. The proposed 1% is Bailey Group’s listing-side brokerage fee. Buyer-broker compensation, closing costs, repairs, and other agreed expenses are separate. A property-specific listing agreement sets the actual terms.

Can I cancel monthly membership?

Yes. The proposed policy is cancellation at any time, with access continuing through the period you’ve paid for and no further renewal. Canceling a subscription does not itself issue a refund.

Is the free buyers list still available?

Yes. You can join the free list without paying for the workshop or membership.

When are the calls?

The weekly schedule and onboarding booking link will be set before enrollment opens. No meeting time has been announced yet.
