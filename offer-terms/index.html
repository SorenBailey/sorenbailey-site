Offer details · Review draft

Know what you’re choosing.

These proposed terms explain the offers in this preview. Enrollment is closed. The final terms will be confirmed before anyone can pay.

The workshop

The Real Estate Scaling Workshop includes a 90-minute recorded video and companion workbook. A paid purchase includes lifetime access. The video and updated workbook are not available yet.

  • Pay now: $97, charged once at checkout.
  • Pay later: card required at checkout, $0 charged that day, then $297 charged once after 48 hours unless canceled before the deadline.
  • Proposed start of the 48 hours: when workshop access is first delivered after checkout, measured in elapsed hours. The account and confirmation email must display the exact date, time, and time zone.
  • Proposed $97 guarantee: if the workshop doesn’t clarify your next move, request a refund within those 48 hours.
  • Pay-later cancellation: cancel within the window and the $297 charge will not be made. There is no payment to refund before a charge occurs.
  • After cancellation or refund: proposed access ends. Paid lifetime access remains for completed, non-refunded purchases.

The owner still needs to confirm the $97 refund window and whether any refund window applies after the deferred $297 charge. Those terms are not final.

The membership

Early Access Buyers includes a one-on-one onboarding call, weekly group Q&A with recordings, and access to member deals 24 hours before the general list. The call schedule will be confirmed before launch. No minimum number of deals, completed purchase, profit, or investment outcome is promised.

  • Monthly: $97 per month, renewing monthly until canceled. Proposed cancellation stops future renewals and keeps access through the paid period. Cancellation is not itself a refund.
  • Standard annual: $996 per year, renewing annually until canceled.
  • Workshop annual offer: $699 per year, a $297 discount from the standard annual price that repeats every year. The owner’s proposed promise is “$699/year for life.” Treatment of cancellation, rejoining, missed payments, and future program changes must be settled before launch.

The 24-hour workshop offer

Proposed start: delivery of the membership invitation after workshop completion. An individual, server-recorded deadline controls eligibility. Merely opening a sales page or returning later will not start or reset the clock.

Proposed eligibility is the same $297 annual discount for either workshop payment option. This needs owner confirmation, particularly for a $97 purchaser or a pay-later attendee whose $297 has not yet been charged.

The draft treats the benefit as a membership discount, not a refund, cash credit, or cancellation of the workshop payment. The annual charge would be separate from any $97 or $297 workshop charge. A refunded or canceled workshop’s effect on eligibility is still open.

The annual guarantee

The proposed annual guarantee requires attending at least one live group call and watching at least two recordings. If the membership still isn’t a fit, the annual payment can be requested back.

Timing is not final. The owner described both a “30-day risk-free guarantee” and requesting a refund “after 30 days.” The current working proposal is to complete those activities during the first 30 days and allow a request on days 30 through 37. This must be confirmed before payment is enabled.

The final policy must also say what happens if a required call or recording is unavailable, and whether the guarantee applies only to the first year or also to renewals. No unqualified risk-free promise is made here.

The $699 purchase credit

The annual offer includes $699 off each eligible deal purchased through Bailey Group, on an ongoing basis. Proposed application is a disclosed credit at closing. It is not cash back and cannot be counted as investment profit.

Before launch, the owner must define eligible transactions and entities, whether the benefit survives membership cancellation, stacking with the $100 hold credit or other concessions, treatment after a membership refund, and approval by the closing team. No purchase credit is issued by this preview.

The $100 property hold

The proposed member benefit is a confirmed 24-hour hold on an eligible property for $100. If the member buys that property, the $100 is credited toward that purchase.

A payment alone will not create a hold. Availability and the ability to hold must be confirmed first. The final terms must define when the 24 hours starts, whether a declined or expired hold is refundable, repeat holds, competing requests, and the credit treatment if a purchase does not close.

A hold is not a purchase contract or earnest-money deposit.

The 1% listing fee and 0% listing guarantee

The proposed member listing fee on flips is 1% for Bailey Group’s listing side. The proposed 0% guarantee waives that listing-side fee when a qualifying purchased deal isn’t a fit and the member asks Bailey Group to list it.

Neither means that every cost of selling is zero. Buyer-broker compensation, closing costs, taxes, repairs, and third-party charges are separate. A property-specific listing agreement controls. There is no guaranteed sale price, sale deadline, profit, or buyback.

The owner still needs to define eligible properties and markets, the deadline and conditions for invoking the 0% guarantee, what “at the end” means, and whether any minimum fee or separate charges apply. These are proposed benefits, not an executed brokerage agreement.

Payment, access, and support

The finished purchase flow will use a secure hosted payment page. Card details will not be collected by this preview. Workshop access must be based on verified payment or verified pay-later enrollment, and membership benefits on verified enrollment.

Before enrollment opens, the purchase account and confirmation email need working cancellation and refund links, payment support details, final business terms, and an accurate privacy notice. No advertising or analytics tracking has been added to this preview.
