Before you start

A few things to know.

Is this for new investors or people who already own property?

Both. The point is to identify your current constraint and your next move. You don’t need to be at a particular door count to work through the workshop.

Is this live?

No. It’s a 90-minute recorded workshop with a companion workbook. You can watch at your own pace during your 48-hour decision window and return to it after purchase.

What happens if I choose pay later?

You enter your card at checkout and get workshop access. No workshop payment is collected that day. Unless you cancel before your exact 48-hour deadline, your card is charged $297 once.

How do I cancel or request my money back?

The planned access email and purchase account will include a cancellation or refund link. The final deadline will be shown there. Enrollment stays closed until this process is working.

Will there be another offer at the end?

Yes. After the workshop, I’ll invite you to the free buyers list. If you want earlier deal access and ongoing support, there’s also an optional paid Early Access membership. Neither is required to get value from the workshop.

Can I buy it yet?

Not yet. This is a preview of the offer. The workshop video, updated workbook, and payment flow are being prepared. No card details or payments are collected here.
